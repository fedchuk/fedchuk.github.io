Spendix

Spendix is a simple expense and budget tracker for Android. Add expenses in one tap by category, set a monthly budget, see how much you can spend per day, track savings goals and recurring payments, and view clear monthly reports. Your data stays on your device.

Spendix Pro

An optional subscription that removes ads and adds automatic daily backup to your own Google Drive, so you can restore your data on any phone.
