Spendix Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Spendix is a personal expense and budget tracker for Android, developed by Maksym Fedchuk. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Your financial data stays on your device

Spendix does not have accounts or its own server. Everything you enter — transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, goals, your name and settings — is stored in the app’s local database on your device. A copy leaves your device only if you turn on Google Drive backup (see below), and then it goes to your own Google Drive. We do not receive, see or sell this data. The anonymous usage statistics described below never include it.

Exchange rates

When you enter an amount in a currency other than your main one, or change your main currency, the app downloads official exchange rates from the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua). The request contains only a date. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that server. No personal or financial data is sent.

Usage analytics

Spendix uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used and to find problems. The app sends anonymous events such as “transaction added”, “report opened” or “export completed”, together with:

  • a random app instance ID created by Firebase (not linked to your name or account),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language, country derived from IP address),
  • simple flags about the action, for example whether a transaction is an expense or income, or whether a foreign currency was used.

Analytics events never contain amounts, transaction titles, notes, category names, your name or any other financial data. The advertising ID is not collected for analytics, and analytics data is not used for advertising or ad personalisation. Data is kept for up to 14 months. Google processes this data on our behalf; see the Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

Spendix shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps) and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Data → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Spendix Pro subscription

Spendix Pro is purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription in order to turn Pro features on. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play at any time.

The app also reads remote settings from Firebase Remote Config, for example how often full-screen ads may be shown. To do this Firebase uses a random installation ID; it contains no personal or financial data.

Ratings and updates

From time to time the app may show the Google Play rating dialog, and it checks Google Play for a newer version that can be downloaded in the background. Both are provided by Google Play on your device; your rating and review are sent to Google Play, not to the developer.

Google Drive backup

Spendix Pro users can turn on automatic backup in Profile → Data → Backup. When you do, the app asks for permission to use the hidden application data folder in your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). This folder is visible only to Spendix and is not shown in your Drive files. The app cannot see or access any other files in your Drive.

Once a day, and when you tap Back up now, the app uploads a file with your transactions, categories, quick amounts, goals, recurring payments and basic settings (currency, budget, theme, name and month start day) to that folder. The three most recent copies are kept and older ones are deleted. You can restore a copy on any phone signed in to the same Google account. The backup goes directly from your device to your Google Drive; the developer has no access to it.

To stop backups, turn off automatic backup in the app. To delete the stored copies and revoke access, open your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps & services → Spendix → Delete all connections, or in Google Drive go to Settings → Manage apps → Spendix → Delete hidden app data.

Spendix’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive is used only to store and restore your Spendix backups and is never shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up the app’s database to your own Google account and restore it on a new device. This backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings; the developer has no access to it.

Export and import

When you choose Export to CSV, the app creates a file with your transactions and opens the system share sheet. Where the file goes is entirely your choice.

When you choose Import from CSV, the app reads only the file you pick and adds its transactions to the local database on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions and categories in the app. To delete all data on your device, clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Google Drive backups are deleted as described in the Google Drive backup section.

Children

Spendix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
