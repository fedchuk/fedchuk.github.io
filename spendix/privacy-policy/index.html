Spendix Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Spendix is a personal expense and budget tracker for Android, developed by Maksym Fedchuk. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Your financial data stays on your device

Spendix does not have accounts, a server or cloud sync. Everything you enter — transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, your name and settings — is stored only in the app’s local database on your device. We do not receive, see or sell this data.

Exchange rates

When you enter an amount in a currency other than your main one, or change your main currency, the app downloads official exchange rates from the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua). The request contains only a date. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that server. No personal or financial data is sent.

Advertising

Spendix shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (impressions and taps) and diagnostic data.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Data → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up the app’s database to your own Google account and restore it on a new device. This backup is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings; the developer has no access to it.

Export

When you choose Export to CSV, the app creates a file with your transactions and opens the system share sheet. Where the file goes is entirely your choice.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions and categories in the app. To delete all data, clear the app’s storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

Children

Spendix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
